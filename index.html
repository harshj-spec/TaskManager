TaskFlow Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to our software design. This Privacy Policy describes how TaskFlow ("we", "our", or "the application") handles your personal data, schedule information, and account credentials.

🛡️ Summary of Key Principles

• Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information or task data to anyone.

• Offline-First Architecture: Your tasks, routines, subtasks, and deadlines remain stored on your local computer.

• Direct Contact: For any privacy questions, contact our data controller directly at harshjhahjh2008@gmail.com.

1. Introduction and Scope

TaskFlow operates as a high-performance productivity and task management web application. This policy applies to all users interacting with the TaskFlow platform. By creating an account or managing tasks within TaskFlow, you acknowledge the data handling practices outlined herein.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We only handle information necessary to provide you with core scheduling functionality:

  • Account Credentials: When you register, we collect your email address and an encrypted hash of your chosen password to facilitate authentication.
  • Task & Schedule Data: Titles, descriptions, categories, priorities, due dates, scheduled times, recurrence settings, and subtask checklist items you enter into the application.
  • Support Communications: If you use the Support & Help feature, we process the problem description and optional contact email you provide to respond to your inquiry.
  • Device Configuration (Client-Side Only): Theme preference (Dark/Light mode) and audio settings, stored locally to maintain your display preferences across browser sessions.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is utilized exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Organizing, sorting, and displaying your daily pending and completed tasks.
  • Calculating real-time deadline proximity and urgency order rankings.
  • Automatically resetting recurring daily routines each morning while preserving historical completion streaks.
  • Facilitating direct email communication when you submit technical support requests to harshjhahjh2008@gmail.com.

4. Local Storage and Client-Side Security

TaskFlow is engineered with an Offline-First & Local-Storage Architecture. All task items, subtasks, and user profiles reside in your browser's local sandbox (localStorage). Your data does not traverse remote commercial servers or third-party cloud data lakes without your direct instruction.

5. Third-Party Disclosures & Tracking

We maintain a Zero-Sale and Zero-Tracking Policy:

  • We do not sell, rent, license, or disclose user data to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing affiliates.
  • We do not utilize tracking pixels, session replay scripts (e.g. Hotjar), or invasive surveillance telemetry.
  • Third-party web services are restricted strictly to foundational CDNs (e.g. Google Fonts and Tailwind CSS) necessary for visual rendering.

6. Data Ownership, Portability & Right to Erasure

You retain absolute ownership of all content entered into TaskFlow. You have the full right to:

  • Export Your Data: Generate a complete machine-readable JSON backup file of all tasks and schedules at any time using the one-click Export tool.
  • Import / Transfer Data: Restore or migrate your data to another device without restrictions.
  • Permanent Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten): Permanently wipe all tasks, user records, and preferences with the "Delete All Tasks" command or by clearing your browser's site cache.

7. Children's Privacy

TaskFlow is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at harshjhahjh2008@gmail.com for immediate deletion.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, performance enhancements, or regulatory adjustments. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of TaskFlow signifies acceptance of any updated terms.
